Before You Choose

What should you check?

Do not rely on a promotional claim alone. Verify important details directly with the relevant regulator and operator.

01. Legal authorization Check whether online gambling is legal where you live and whether the operator is authorized there.
02. Licence verification Look for the licence number and verify it through the official regulator's website.
03. Bonus conditions Read wagering rules, eligible games, expiry dates and withdrawal restrictions.
04. Payments and fees Check supported payment methods, minimum withdrawals, fees and processing conditions.
05. Account protection Review privacy policies, security measures and the identity verification process.
06. Country restrictions Confirm that the service accepts residents of your country under applicable laws.

Gambling involves financial risk

Gambling can lead to financial loss and harm. Never gamble with money needed for food, housing, bills or other essentials. Do not borrow to gamble or chase losses. Consider setting strict limits, taking breaks and using self-exclusion tools where available.
